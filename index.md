  <h1>CourierMap</h1>
  <p class="lede"><strong>A driver-only shop rating map for delivery couriers.</strong></p>
  <div class="hero-copy">
    <p>CourierMap is a map app with a shop rating system that drivers can contribute to; separate from main-stream map apps such as Google Maps or Apple Maps.</p>
    <p>The idea is simple: when you are at a shop for pickup, you can see what other drivers have said about the shop, so you can decide whether to <strong>wait or cancel</strong> instead of guessing based on real history which is the rating based on other drivers.</p>
    <p>This is especially useful when you're new to an area and don't yet know which shops are reliable and which ones will leave you waiting, especially if you are in a rush to complete a quest.</p>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="features">
  <p class="features-lead">When you arrive at a new shop, you can pull up CourierMap and instantly see:</p>
  <ul>
    <li>
      <span class="glyph" aria-hidden="true">⭐</span>
      <span><strong>Average wait time, ratings &amp; comments</strong> left by other drivers so if a place is known for long waits, you can cancel and protect a quest or leave your own review so that other drivers can see and adjust their hourly target and quests instead of getting stuck.</span>
    </li>
    <li>
      <span class="glyph" aria-hidden="true">📍</span>
      <span><strong>Pickup tips</strong> driver notes on navigating tricky locations: multi-level malls, hidden takeaway-only spots, where to actually park, which entrance to use.</span>
    </li>
  </ul>
  <p class="features-close">It's the kind of local knowledge drivers usually trade in group chats now collected in one place, pinned to the actual shop on a map.</p>
</section>

<section class="audience">
  <h2>Who it's for</h2>
  <p>Food delivery drivers (Uber, DoorDash, HungryPanda, and others) who want to make faster, smarter decisions at pickup. Ratings are contributed by drivers, for drivers.</p>
</section>
